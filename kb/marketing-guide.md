# Marketing Your eBook — Complete Guide

This guide covers proven strategies to promote your eBook, build an audience, and maximize sales without spending a dime on advertising.

## Phase 1: Pre-Launch (2 Weeks Before)

**Build Anticipation:**
- Share teaser excerpts on social media (Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram)
- Start a countdown on your personal accounts
- Create a simple landing page with a signup form for early readers
- Reach out to 10-20 people in your network who would genuinely enjoy the book

**Build Your Launch Team:**
- Recruit beta readers from relevant communities
- Offer a free copy in exchange for an honest review
- Create a private group (Discord, WhatsApp) for your launch team

## Phase 2: Launch Week

**Day 1 — Announce:**
- Email your list with a compelling subject line
- Post on all social platforms with a graphic
- Announce in relevant online communities (be respectful of rules)

**Day 2-3 — Amplify:**
- Share behind-the-scenes content about your writing process
- Post quotes and key insights from the book
- Engage with every comment and share

**Day 4-7 — Sustain:**
- Share reader testimonials and early reviews
- Post a "making of" thread or video
- Reach out to podcasters and bloggers in your niche

## Phase 3: Long-Term Growth

**Content Marketing:**
- Write blog posts related to your book's topic
- Create short-form content (threads, carousels, shorts)
- Guest post on established blogs in your niche

**Community Building:**
- Start a newsletter with valuable content
- Engage daily in relevant online communities
- Respond to every review and message personally

**Free Promotion Channels:**
- Virtus Publishing featured placement
- Reddit: r/selfpublish, r/writing, niche-specific subreddits
- Twitter/X #WritingCommunity
- LinkedIn articles (especially for non-fiction)
- Goodreads giveaways
- BookBub featured deals (when eligible)

## Pricing Strategy

- **New authors:** Start at $0.99-$2.99 to build reviews
- **Established authors:** $4.99-$9.99 for full-length works
- **Premium content:** $9.99-$14.99 for comprehensive guides
- **Free periods:** Use strategically to boost visibility and reviews

## Measuring Success

Track these metrics monthly:
- Sales volume and revenue
- Review count and average rating
- Email list growth
- Social media engagement rate
- Website/store traffic
