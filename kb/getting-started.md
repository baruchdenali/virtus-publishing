# Getting Started with Virtus Publishing

Welcome to Virtus Publishing — the AI-powered eBook creation and publishing platform designed for serious authors. This guide walks you through everything you need to create, publish, and sell your first eBook.

## Creating Your Account

Sign up at the login page with your email address. You will be assigned the appropriate role based on your email domain. Company emails (virtuspublishing.us or virtus-edu.net) are automatically granted team roles.

## Choosing Your Membership

Virtus Publishing offers four membership tiers:

- **Creator ($29/mo)** — 5 AI eBooks/month, basic covers, standard distribution
- **Professional ($49/mo)** — 25 AI eBooks/month, advanced covers, premium distribution, marketing toolkit
- **Publisher ($149/mo)** — Unlimited eBooks, audiobook narration, global distribution, API access
- **Enterprise (Custom)** — White-label, SSO, custom AI training, dedicated support

## Creating Your First eBook

Navigate to Create eBook from your dashboard. You have two options:

**Start from Scratch** — Our AI assistant helps you outline, write, and refine your eBook chapter by chapter.

**Upload Manuscript** — Drag and drop your existing manuscript (TXT, PDF, DOCX). We extract the text, auto-detect your title, and prep it for editing.

## The AI Assistant

Virtus AI runs on your local machine via Ollama — no API keys, no cloud costs, no data leaving your system. Install Ollama and run a model to activate real AI responses. Without Ollama, the assistant provides enhanced template responses.

## Publishing and Selling

Once your eBook is complete, click Publish in the editor. Set your price or make it free. Your eBook appears in the Virtus Store where readers can purchase it. You keep 80% of every sale.
